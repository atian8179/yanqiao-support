提交反馈 / Open a support request

启用键盘

打开 iPhone「设置 → 通用 → 键盘 → 键盘 → 添加新键盘」，选择言桥。在任意输入框中长按地球键，切换到言桥。

准备翻译语言

在言桥 App 的「语言准备」中选择需要的语言，按系统提示下载资源。初次下载需要网络；可用语言以设备和系统提供的资源为准。翻译使用 Apple 设备端能力。

完全访问与本机记忆

基础中文输入无需开启完全访问。完全访问用于键盘扩展的系统能力与本机共享设置，不代表上传文字。本机联想记忆可在键盘设置的「更多」中清空。

English support

YanQiao is a Chinese keyboard for iPhone, with full Pinyin and nine-key layouts, phrase suggestions, emoji and categorized symbols. It also offers on-device translation, pronunciation and the option to insert a translation into your current text field. The app interface is primarily in Chinese.

To enable it, open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and select YanQiao. Hold the globe key in a text field to switch keyboards. Prepare the desired translation languages in the app first; initial language downloads require an internet connection. Availability depends on your device and Apple's language support.

No account is required. The first release is free, with no ads or in-app purchases. Basic Chinese typing works without Full Access. For help, open a support request. Please do not include sensitive personal information in public reports.
