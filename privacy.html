隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 5 日

言桥不要求注册账号，没有广告，不接入广告追踪或第三方统计服务。我们不收集或上传你的输入文字、译文或完整聊天记录。

输入与本机记忆

键盘为选词、联想和翻译处理当前输入及必要的临时上下文。确认过的短词和常用搭配可保存在本机，帮助后续输入。这部分联想记忆不上传、不备份到 iCloud，可在键盘设置的「更多」中清空。

翻译与朗读

翻译使用 Apple 设备端能力。首次使用某种语言可能需要从 Apple 下载语言资源；资源准备完成后，文字翻译在设备上处理。可用语言由系统和设备支持情况决定。朗读使用系统语音能力。言桥没有自建云端翻译接口。

剪贴板、完全访问与偏好设置

言桥不自动读取剪贴板，仅在你主动粘贴时处理相应内容。基础中文输入不要求「允许完全访问」。完全访问是系统对键盘扩展的能力授权，用于本机共享设置等能力，不代表同意上传文字。你可在系统设置中随时关闭。语言、布局和触感偏好保存在本机。

数据管理和联系

可清空键盘本机联想记忆，或删除应用和键盘。如有隐私问题，请通过支持页面联系我们。公开反馈时请勿包含私人聊天、密码或其他敏感信息。支持网站由 GitHub 托管，访问网站适用 GitHub 隐私政策。本政策的重要更新将在此页公布。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

YanQiao does not require an account, display advertisements, or include advertising trackers or third-party analytics. We do not collect or upload your typed text, translations, or complete conversations.

Typing and local learning

The keyboard processes current input and the temporary context needed for candidates, suggestions and translation. Short words and common combinations you confirm may be stored locally to improve future input. This suggestion memory is not uploaded or backed up to iCloud. You can clear it in the keyboard settings under “更多” (More).

Translation and speech

Translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework. A language may require an initial resource download from Apple. Once resources are ready, text translation is processed on the device. Supported languages depend on your device and operating system. Pronunciation uses system speech services. YanQiao does not operate a cloud translation endpoint.

Clipboard, Full Access and settings

YanQiao does not automatically read your clipboard. It processes pasted content only when you explicitly paste. Basic Chinese typing does not require Full Access. Full Access is an iOS keyboard capability permission used for capabilities such as sharing local preferences; it is not consent to upload text. You can disable it in iOS Settings. Language, layout and haptic preferences are stored locally.

Your choices and contact

You may clear local suggestion memory or remove the app and keyboard. For privacy questions, please contact us through the support page. Do not post private conversations, passwords or other sensitive information in public reports. GitHub hosts this support website; visiting it is subject to GitHub's privacy policy. Material policy updates will appear on this page.
